What is Operational Data Sharing?
=================================

Downlink satellite interference at certain frequencies has the
potential to degrade GBT data quality and to possibly damage
electronic components in certain receivers.  Opertional Data Sharing
(ODS) is an autonomous reporting system for mitigating some of this
satellite interference. A more detailed description of ODS can be
found on the `NRAO ODS webpage
<https://science.nrao.edu/facilities/vla/docs/manuals/obsguide/ods-system>`_ .

The use ODS mitigation is **required** for some GBT receivers, and
recommended for others.  See :ref:`receivers` for an up-to-date list
of receivers for which ODS is required and recommended.

**Observing teams MUST adhere to** `GBO policies <https://greenbankobservatory.org/portal/policies/>`_ **regarding preparing their scripts and catalogs in advance and  certifying that their projects and scripts/catalogs are ready to  observe.**

GBT observers should consult :ref:`ods-instructions` for detailed
instructions on how to use ODS.

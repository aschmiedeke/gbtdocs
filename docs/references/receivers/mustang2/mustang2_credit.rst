#########################
Credit for MUSTANG-2 Data
#########################

Who should be included on publications if you make use of MUSTANG-2 data?
	- The :ref:`M2 instrument team <mustang2_team>` should be given the opportunity to opt-in as co-author. Send a copy of your paper to mustangsz - at - googlegroups - dot - com alerting the team to the publication, asking for people to opt-in, and tell them the deadline to opt-in/send comments.
	- Further, the M2 instrument team members should use the following as a guide for their opt in: if they have observed (at least once) for the project or contributed to observing in some way (i.e., tuning, observing, observer training, etc.) or the data reduction or analysis in some way (i.e., calibration of the data, data reduction, etc.) or if were involved in the design, building, commissioning, or up-keep of the instrument. The PI or M2 team member can check the observing logs to determine if they contributed to the observing (tuning or observing).

Please also include the following acknowledgement in your publication if you have used MUSTANG-2 data: “The National Radio Astronomy Observatory (NRAO) and the Green Bank Observatory (GBO) are facilities of the National Science Foundation (NSF) operated under cooperative agreement by Associated Universities, Inc."

In the section of the paper where you describe the M2 observations that were used, include: 
	- the project number(s) and PI(s) of the observations (e.g., AGBT23B\_005 (PI: Moravec))
	- a citation to https://scixplorer.org/abs/2014SPIE.9153E..0JD/abstract

Data analysis tools:
	- If you used minkasi for your analysis, please include the following acknowledgment in your paper, “We used minkasi for this work and acknowledge the work of Jonathan Sievers, John Orlowski-Scherer, and Saianeesh Haridas in developing minkasi.”
	- If you used WITCH for your analysis, please include the following acknowledgment in your paper, “We used WITCH for this work and acknowledge the work of John Orlowski-Scherer, Saianeesh Haridas, Luca Di Mascolo, Eleonora Barbavara, Jonathan Sievers, and Rebecca Dudley in developing WITCH.” Also consider offering authorship to those listed depending on the use of WITCH and if there was work done on WITCH to analyze your science
